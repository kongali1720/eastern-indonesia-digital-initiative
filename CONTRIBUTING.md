# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in the Eastern Indonesia Digital Education
& Data Center Initiative.

## How to Contribute

1. Open an issue describing the proposed improvement.
2. Explain the rationale, scope, assumptions, and potential risks.
3. Submit changes through a focused pull request.
4. Keep documentation clear, verifiable, and professional.
5. Do not publish confidential, personal, financial, or security-sensitive
   information.

## Project Standards

- Distinguish verified facts from assumptions and proposals.
- Cite authoritative sources for regulatory, technical, and financial claims.
- Do not present preliminary budgets as approved funding.
- Do not imply that partnerships, approvals, or investments exist unless
  they have been formally confirmed.
- Avoid including credentials, private keys, access tokens, or personal data.

## Pull Requests

Include a concise summary, rationale, affected files, validation performed,
and any remaining limitations.

Contributions may be reviewed before acceptance. Submission does not
guarantee acceptance or transfer ownership beyond the applicable legal terms.
