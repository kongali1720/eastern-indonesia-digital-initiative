# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We aim to maintain a respectful, constructive, and inclusive environment
for everyone participating in this project.

## Expected Behavior

- Communicate respectfully and professionally.
- Provide constructive feedback.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality.
- Distinguish evidence from assumptions.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, and personal attacks.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, intimidation, deliberate disclosure of confidential information,
and abusive conduct are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the project owner through a verified contact
channel. Reports will be reviewed as appropriate to the project's capacity.
