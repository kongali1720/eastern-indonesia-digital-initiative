# Legal and Compliance Checklist

This checklist is for planning and does not constitute legal advice or
confirmation that any permit has been granted.

- [ ] Confirm the appropriate legal entity and ownership structure.
- [ ] Obtain written guidance from the competent education authority.
- [ ] Confirm current requirements for establishment and operation of an SMA.
- [ ] Verify land title, zoning, building requirements, and permitted use.
- [ ] Confirm building, fire, health, sanitation, and accessibility requirements.
- [ ] Review teacher qualifications, staffing, and safeguarding policies.
- [ ] Validate curriculum and student assessment requirements.
- [ ] Prepare student data protection and cybersecurity procedures.
- [ ] Review employment, tax, accounting, and reporting obligations.
- [ ] Document investor, donor, scholarship, and procurement agreements.
- [ ] Obtain all required approvals before commencing regulated activities.

Record each requirement with the responsible owner, authority, evidence,
submission date, status, and next action.
