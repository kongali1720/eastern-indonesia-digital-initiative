# Separate Data Center Investment Case

## Planning Envelope
IDR 1.1 trillion — preliminary planning figure only; not a verified budget,
financing commitment, valuation, or approved expenditure.

## Financial Separation
Maintain a separate project charter, feasibility study, financial model,
bank accounts where appropriate, contracts, risk register, approvals,
and investor reporting.

## Required Studies
- Demand, customer pipeline, and capacity plan.
- Power availability, cost, redundancy, and energy strategy.
- Connectivity, fiber routes, and carrier diversity.
- Site, climate, physical security, and environmental requirements.
- Cooling, fire suppression, backup power, and resilience.
- Hardware, software, staffing, operations, and maintenance.
- Regulatory, data protection, cybersecurity, and disaster recovery.
- Capital expenditure, operating expenditure, funding, and sensitivity analysis.

Do not transfer education-project funds to this workstream without
documented authorization, legal review, and transparent accounting.
