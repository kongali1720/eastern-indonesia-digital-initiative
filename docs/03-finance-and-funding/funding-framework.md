# Finance and Funding Framework

## Separate Financial Workstreams
1. SMA IT establishment and operations.
2. Future university pathway.
3. Digital platform and school IT systems.
4. Data center project, managed as a separate investment case.

## SMA Financial Model
Prepare separate schedules for:
- Land and site preparation.
- Design, permits, and professional services.
- Construction and facilities.
- Furniture, laboratory, and IT equipment.
- Recruitment and pre-opening costs.
- Salaries, utilities, maintenance, insurance, and administration.
- Curriculum, student support, and scholarships.
- Contingency, cash runway, and working capital.

## Revenue and Funding Scenarios
Model tuition revenue, scholarship sponsorship, grants, founder funding,
and investor funding separately. Do not count uncommitted funding as cash.

## Required Controls
- Annual budget approved by authorized management.
- Monthly budget-versus-actual report.
- Bank reconciliation and supporting documentation.
- Purchase approval and conflict-of-interest declarations.
- Restricted-fund tracking for scholarships.
- Documented investor terms and use-of-funds reporting.

## Financial Model Status
No final budget should be approved until local cost estimates, land details,
staffing assumptions, and vendor quotations have been validated.
