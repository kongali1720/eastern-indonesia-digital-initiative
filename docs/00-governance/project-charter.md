# Project Charter

## Purpose
Develop a phased digital education initiative for Eastern Indonesia,
starting with an SMA IT project in Gowa, South Sulawesi.

## Strategic Sequence
1. SMA IT feasibility and establishment preparation.
2. Campus operations, curriculum, staffing, and scholarship delivery.
3. Evaluation of a future university pathway.
4. Digital infrastructure integration where technically and financially justified.

## Initial Planning Parameters
- Candidate location: Gowa, South Sulawesi, Indonesia.
- Planned SMA capacity: 540 students at maturity.
- Illustrative capacity: 3 grade levels x 6 classes x 30 students.
- Funding concept: founder contribution, investors or partners, and scholarship funding.
- Digital infrastructure reference: Kongali Server dashboard.
- Separate data center planning envelope: IDR 1.1 trillion.

These are planning parameters, not proof of land availability, permits,
committed investment, vendor pricing, or project approval.

## Governance Principles
- Separate education and data center budgets.
- Record assumptions and verified facts separately.
- Require documented approval for material spending.
- Maintain transparent investor, scholarship, and financial records.
- Do not promise returns, enrollment, or regulatory approval without evidence.

## Decision Gates
1. Concept approval.
2. Feasibility and demand validation.
3. Legal, land, and licensing review.
4. Funding commitment and approved budget.
5. Construction and operational readiness.
6. Opening approval by the competent authorities.
