# Integrated Education Pathway

## Strategic Concept

The initiative may connect formal secondary education, short-course
training, further study, and digital infrastructure through a coherent
long-term strategy.

Integration should mean aligned learning pathways and shared standards
where appropriate. It must not mean automatically combining budgets,
legal responsibilities, student records, or regulatory permissions.

## Proposed Pathway

1. SMA IT provides foundational academic and digital education.
2. The course institution offers optional short courses and practical
   skills development for eligible learners.
3. Graduates may pursue university, vocational study, employment,
   entrepreneurship, or further training.
4. A future university project is assessed only after a separate
   feasibility and regulatory review.
5. Digital infrastructure supports learning under documented security,
   privacy, and operating controls.

## Shared Capabilities to Evaluate

- Learning resources and digital content.
- IT support and cybersecurity standards.
- Career guidance and employer engagement.
- Instructor development.
- Procurement and vendor management.
- Reporting and performance measurement.

Shared services require clear service agreements, cost allocation,
data-access rules, and accountable owners.

## Legal and Financial Separation

Each institution or project must have a documented legal basis,
governance structure, budget, contracts, and reporting responsibilities
appropriate to its status and applicable law.
