# Change Control

## Purpose
Ensure changes to project scope, cost, schedule, technology, location,
or intended outcomes are evaluated before implementation.

## Change request requirements
Every request should include:
- Unique change ID and requester.
- Description and reason for change.
- Affected deliverables and workstreams.
- Schedule impact and dependencies.
- CAPEX, OPEX, cash-flow, and funding impact.
- Risk, legal, security, and environmental implications.
- Options considered and recommendation.
- Required approvals and implementation owner.

## Change workflow
1. Register the request.
2. Check completeness and classify materiality.
3. Assess cross-workstream and financial impacts.
4. Obtain technical, finance, legal, and risk reviews as relevant.
5. Submit to the authorized decision-maker.
6. Record approved, rejected, deferred, or withdrawn status.
7. Update baselines and notify affected workstream owners.
8. Verify implementation and close with evidence.

## Controls
No request changes the approved baseline until formally authorized.
Emergency exceptions must follow an approved emergency procedure
and be documented retrospectively within the required period.

Any proposed revision to the master investment envelope must show
the original baseline, proposed change, reason, funding source,
and explicit approval status.
