# Project Controls

## Objective
Establish one consistent method for managing scope, schedule, cost,
deliverables, issues, and dependencies.

## Minimum project-control fields
Every work package should identify:
- Unique work package ID.
- Workstream and deliverable.
- Accountable owner and supporting parties.
- Scope and acceptance criteria.
- Planned start and finish dates.
- Current forecast dates.
- Dependencies and prerequisites.
- Approved budget and forecast cost, where applicable.
- Status, evidence, risks, and next action.

## Status definitions
- Not Started: work has not begun.
- In Progress: work is underway with evidence of activity.
- At Risk: an agreed outcome may not be achieved.
- Blocked: a dependency or decision prevents progress.
- Complete: acceptance criteria have been verified.
- On Hold: authorized temporary suspension.
- Cancelled: formally approved closure.

## Variance management
Record the baseline, current forecast, variance, cause, impact,
owner, mitigation, and escalation date for material deviations.

A forecast is not an approved baseline change.

## Reporting rules
- Date-stamp each report.
- Identify its reporting period and preparer.
- Link supporting evidence where available.
- Distinguish actual expenditure from commitments and forecasts.
- Explain missing information rather than silently assuming zero.
- Escalate overdue high-impact actions to the designated authority.

## Financial reconciliation
Reconcile workstream estimates against the master investment budget
and integrated financial model. Avoid double-counting shared assets,
contingency, and the data-center allocation.

The IDR 2.7 trillion envelope is preliminary and unverified.
The IDR 1.1 trillion data-center allocation is included within it.

## Corrective actions
Each material issue should have a named owner, due date, proposed
resolution, and closure evidence.
