# Project Management Office (PMO)

## Purpose
Establish a coordinated project-control function across all workstreams
of the Eastern Indonesia Digital Education & Data Center Initiative.

## Responsibilities
- Maintain the integrated master schedule and milestone baseline.
- Assign accountable owners to deliverables and decisions.
- Track scope, schedule, cost, dependencies, and delivery risks.
- Coordinate reporting across education, data center, energy,
  infrastructure, finance, and community workstreams.
- Maintain decision, issue, assumption, and change registers.
- Escalate material delays, cost variances, and unresolved dependencies.
- Prepare management reviews using documented evidence.

## Governance boundaries
The PMO coordinates and reports; it does not independently authorize
unapproved expenditure, contracts, financing, construction, or changes
to the approved project scope.

Approvals must follow the formally adopted delegation-of-authority
framework and applicable legal requirements.

## Reporting cadence
- Weekly: workstream status, immediate blockers, and upcoming milestones.
- Monthly: consolidated schedule, budget, risks, decisions, and changes.
- At each stage gate: evidence review and documented go/no-go decision.

Cadence may be adjusted by the authorized project sponsor.

## Core controls
1. Every deliverable has an owner and acceptance criteria.
2. Every milestone has a baseline date and current forecast.
3. Every material variance has an explanation and corrective action.
4. Every change has impact assessment and recorded approval.
5. Every decision has an owner, date, rationale, and evidence reference.
6. Reports distinguish verified facts from estimates and assumptions.

## Key performance indicators
- Milestones achieved on time.
- Deliverables accepted against agreed criteria.
- Overdue high-priority actions.
- Open decisions past due.
- Changes awaiting approval.
- Forecast versus approved budget.
- Material risks without an assigned response owner.
