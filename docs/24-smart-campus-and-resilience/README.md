# Smart Campus and Regional Resilience

## Purpose
Plan a safe, accessible, efficient, and resilient campus environment
that supports education and essential digital services.

## Proposed scope
- Campus access control and physical security.
- Building and facility maintenance management.
- Environmental and utility monitoring.
- Emergency communications and incident procedures.
- Disaster-risk and site-hazard assessment.
- Accessible facilities and inclusive campus design.
- Waste management and environmental stewardship.
- Continuity planning for critical campus services.

## Planning principles
Use privacy-conscious monitoring, clear retention rules, appropriate
access controls, and human oversight for consequential decisions.

Evaluate local hazards, evacuation routes, emergency access, utility
dependencies, and continuity arrangements before site development.

## Key performance indicators
- Safety inspection and maintenance completion.
- Emergency drill completion and corrective actions.
- Critical utility and service interruptions.
- Accessibility review findings resolved.
- Waste-management and environmental compliance.
- Continuity-plan and recovery-test completion.

## Gate conditions
Obtain site-specific engineering, environmental, accessibility,
safety, and regulatory assessments before finalizing campus design.
