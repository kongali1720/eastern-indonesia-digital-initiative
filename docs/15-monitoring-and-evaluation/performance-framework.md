# Monitoring and Evaluation Framework

## Purpose
Measure whether each workstream is progressing against approved
educational, financial, operational, and governance objectives.

## SMA IT Indicators
- Enrollment by grade.
- Attendance and retention.
- Learning outcomes and progression.
- Teacher recruitment and retention.
- Student safeguarding and wellbeing indicators.
- Scholarship awards and funded commitments.
- Budget variance and cash runway.
- Facility and IT availability.

## Course Institution Indicators
- Enrollment by course.
- Attendance and completion.
- Assessment results and portfolio completion.
- Learner satisfaction.
- Repeat enrollment and partner feedback.
- Cost per learner and course contribution.

## Governance Indicators
- Timeliness of financial reporting.
- Bank reconciliations completed.
- Procurement approvals documented.
- Material risks reviewed.
- Legal and regulatory actions tracked.
- Corrective actions closed by due date.

## Data Quality
Every indicator must have a definition, owner, source, reporting
frequency, target rationale, and data-quality check.

Avoid collecting personal information that is not necessary for the
indicator. Limit access to identifiable student and learner data.
