# Implementation Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Definition
- Confirm project owner and governance.
- Create the evidence register.
- Identify candidate sites and stakeholder groups.
- Approve the scope of the feasibility study.

## Phase 2 — Feasibility
- Validate local demand and affordability.
- Obtain site and construction assessments.
- Develop academic, staffing, and operating plans.
- Prepare scenario-based financial projections.
- Obtain regulatory guidance.

## Phase 3 — Investment and Readiness
- Secure documented funding commitments.
- Finalize the site and legal due diligence.
- Obtain required approvals.
- Approve procurement and construction plans.
- Recruit leadership and prepare operating policies.

## Phase 4 — Launch Readiness
- Verify facilities and safety requirements.
- Confirm staffing, curriculum, student services, and IT readiness.
- Test financial controls and incident response.
- Open only when all applicable approvals and readiness criteria are met.

## Phase 5 — Review
- Monitor enrollment, finances, academic quality, scholarships, and risks.
- Conduct periodic management and stakeholder reviews.
