# SMA IT Project Concept

## Objective
Assess the feasibility of establishing an information-technology-oriented
senior secondary school in Gowa, South Sulawesi.

## Target Capacity
540 students at maturity:
- Grade X: 6 classes x 30 students.
- Grade XI: 6 classes x 30 students.
- Grade XII: 6 classes x 30 students.

The capacity is a planning target, not a confirmed enrollment forecast.

## Proposed Learning Areas
- National curriculum requirements.
- Mathematics, sciences, languages, and social studies.
- Computer science and programming fundamentals.
- Digital literacy, cybersecurity awareness, and data literacy.
- Entrepreneurship, project work, and responsible technology use.
- Character development, safeguarding, and student wellbeing.

The final curriculum must comply with current Indonesian education rules
and be reviewed by qualified education professionals.

## Student Access
Consider a mixed model:
- Fee-paying students.
- Need-based or merit-based scholarship students.
- Sponsor-funded scholarships, subject to written agreements.

Scholarship eligibility, selection, renewal, and funding rules must be
documented before recruitment.

## Required Feasibility Work
- Local demographic and school-demand study.
- Parent and student surveys.
- Competitor and tuition benchmarking.
- Land, access, utilities, and safety assessment.
- Staffing and operating-cost model.
- Legal entity, school licensing, and curriculum review.

## Go/No-Go Criteria
Proceed only after demand, licensing, funding, site suitability, staffing,
and operating sustainability have been independently reviewed.
