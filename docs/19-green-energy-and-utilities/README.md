# Green Energy and Smart Utilities

## Purpose
Develop a resilient, efficient, and environmentally responsible utility
framework for the proposed education campus and regional data center.

## Proposed scope
- Solar photovoltaic feasibility assessment.
- Battery storage and backup-power assessment.
- Grid connection and power-quality analysis.
- Data-center cooling and energy-efficiency strategy.
- Water supply, wastewater, and rainwater management.
- Energy monitoring and utility-cost reporting.
- Equipment lifecycle and electronic-waste management.

## Integration
Energy planning must coordinate with:
- Data-center capacity and availability requirements.
- Campus buildings, laboratories, and classrooms.
- Digital infrastructure and cybersecurity operations.
- Campus resilience and emergency response.

## Required studies
1. Load forecast by facility and implementation phase.
2. Grid capacity, reliability, and connection-cost assessment.
3. Renewable-energy yield and lifecycle-cost analysis.
4. Backup-power design and maintenance requirements.
5. Water availability and environmental assessment.
6. Applicable permits, technical standards, and safety requirements.

## Key performance indicators
- Energy consumption per facility.
- Energy cost per operational unit.
- Renewable energy contribution, where installed.
- Backup-power test compliance.
- Cooling efficiency and equipment availability.
- Water consumption and waste-management compliance.

## Gate conditions
No equipment procurement or construction commitment should be made
before engineering studies, vendor validation, funding approval,
permitting, and lifecycle-cost review are complete.

All technologies and installations described here are proposals,
not confirmed facilities.
