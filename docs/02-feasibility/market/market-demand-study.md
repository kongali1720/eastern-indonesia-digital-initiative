# Market and Demand Study

## Objective
Determine whether sufficient demand exists for the proposed SMA IT
and digital skills course institution in Gowa and the surrounding area.

## Research Questions
- Which student and family segments are underserved?
- Which schools and training providers already serve these segments?
- What are prevailing tuition and course prices?
- Which digital skills are valued by local employers?
- What are the main barriers to enrollment?
- How much demand is likely at different price points?
- Which scholarship model would improve access sustainably?

## Methods
- Review official demographic and education statistics.
- Map competing institutions and course providers.
- Conduct structured parent and student surveys.
- Interview employers and community stakeholders.
- Benchmark tuition, facilities, programs, and admissions.
- Test course concepts and proposed schedules.

## Evidence Rules
Document the survey instrument, sample, collection dates, limitations,
and analysis method. Distinguish stated interest from willingness to pay
and actual enrollment.

## Deliverable
A dated report containing demand estimates, competitor analysis,
price sensitivity, target segments, limitations, and recommendations.
