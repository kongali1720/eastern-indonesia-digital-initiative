# Financial Feasibility Model Framework

## Separate Models
Maintain independent financial models for SMA IT, the course institution,
the future university pathway, shared education technology, and the
data center.

## CAPEX Categories
- Land acquisition or lawful site-use arrangements.
- Professional fees, surveys, and design.
- Permits and required assessments.
- Construction, fit-out, and utilities.
- Furniture and teaching equipment.
- Computer laboratories and network equipment.
- Initial software and implementation costs.
- Pre-opening recruitment and training.
- Contingency based on documented project risks.

## OPEX Categories
- Salaries and benefits.
- Teaching materials and academic operations.
- Utilities, internet, and maintenance.
- Software, hosting, security, and support.
- Insurance, administration, and compliance.
- Student services and scholarship commitments.
- Marketing, recruitment, and admissions.
- Equipment replacement and other recurring costs.

## Revenue Categories
- Tuition and permitted education fees.
- Course tuition and training contracts.
- Signed sponsorships and grants.
- Other permitted revenue supported by evidence.

Record expected revenue separately from contracted or received funds.

## Core Outputs
- Annual profit-and-loss projection.
- Monthly cash-flow projection during establishment.
- Annual cash-flow projection during operations.
- Break-even enrollment and price sensitivity.
- Working-capital requirement.
- Funding gap and timing.
- Downside, base, and upside scenarios.
- Sensitivity to cost and enrollment changes.

## Planning Estimates
Any preliminary SMA CAPEX or OPEX ranges must be labeled unverified
until supported by local quotations, scope, site information, and a
documented estimating method.

## Data Center
The IDR 1.1 trillion planning envelope is a separate unverified input.
Do not add it to education CAPEX or assume it is available funding.
