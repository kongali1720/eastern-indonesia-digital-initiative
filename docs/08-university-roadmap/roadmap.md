# Future University Pathway

## Position
The university pathway is a later-stage strategic option. The initial
execution priority remains SMA IT feasibility and readiness.

## Stage Gates
1. Demonstrate a sustainable SMA operating model.
2. Review academic outcomes, demand, governance, and finances.
3. Conduct a separate higher-education feasibility study.
4. Obtain written regulatory guidance on the proposed institution and programs.
5. Assess qualified academic leadership, faculty, facilities, and funding.
6. Proceed only through the applicable legal establishment and licensing route.

A partnership with an existing institution may be explored, but it does not
automatically authorize the project to issue degrees or operate a campus.
