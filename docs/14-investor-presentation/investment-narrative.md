# Investor and Strategic Partner Narrative

## Project Thesis

The Eastern Indonesia Digital Education Initiative is a proposed,
phased investment in education and practical digital skills. Its initial
focus is an SMA IT project in Gowa, supported by a possible digital
skills course institution and a long-term pathway toward further study.

## Investment Principles

- Evidence-led demand assessment.
- Phased capital deployment.
- Transparent governance and reporting.
- Clear separation of education and data center finances.
- Student protection and responsible data use.
- Measurable educational and operational outcomes.
- Documented funding commitments and use of funds.

## What Investors Should Receive

- Project scope and implementation stages.
- Market evidence and research limitations.
- Site and technical assessments.
- Legal and regulatory status.
- CAPEX and OPEX evidence.
- Scenario-based financial projections.
- Funding requirements and conditions.
- Governance and reporting arrangements.
- Risk register and mitigation owners.
- Milestones and decision gates.

## Claims Policy

Do not claim guaranteed returns, confirmed enrollment, secured land,
approved permits, or committed capital unless documentary evidence
supports the statement.

Any investment terms must be reviewed by qualified legal and financial
advisers before presentation as an offer.
