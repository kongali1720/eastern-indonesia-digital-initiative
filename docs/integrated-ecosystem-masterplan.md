# Integrated Ecosystem Masterplan

## Purpose
This document connects education, digital infrastructure, regional
economic development, talent programs, and campus resilience into one
coordinated planning framework for the proposed initiative in Gowa,
South Sulawesi.

## Integration rules
1. Governance, feasibility, legal review, and funding approval apply to every workstream.
2. Shared infrastructure must be planned against validated demand and operating costs.
3. New initiatives must identify dependencies, accountable owners, measurable outcomes, and risks.
4. Pilots should precede large-scale commitments where uncertainty remains.
5. Budget changes must be reconciled with the existing master investment budget.
6. KPI reports must distinguish measured results from targets and projections.

## Budget boundary
The existing IDR 2.7 trillion master investment envelope is a preliminary,
unverified planning assumption. The IDR 1.1 trillion data-center allocation
is included in that total.

The six workstreams introduced here do not automatically add funding to
the total. Any new cost must be estimated, reconciled against existing
allocations, and approved through the project's governance process.

## Implementation gates
- Gate 1: Demand, site, technical, environmental, and regulatory validation.
- Gate 2: Governance approval, financing evidence, and operating model.
- Gate 3: Detailed design, procurement readiness, and risk review.
- Gate 4: Controlled pilots and acceptance testing.
- Gate 5: Operational evaluation and evidence-based expansion decision.

## Status
Conceptual planning framework. This document does not represent
construction approval, committed investment, regulatory authorization,
or confirmation that proposed facilities exist.
