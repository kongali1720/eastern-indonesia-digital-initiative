# Scholarship and Talent Development

## Purpose
Create fairer access to digital education and develop a skilled
regional workforce.

## Proposed programs
- Need-based and merit-based scholarships.
- Digital skills courses and professional certification pathways.
- Teacher training and digital teaching resources.
- Mentorship, internships, and career guidance.
- Outreach for underserved and remote communities.
- Graduate tracking and employer feedback.

## Scholarship governance
Publish eligibility criteria, application procedures, selection
rubrics, award values, renewal conditions, appeals, and reporting rules.

Use a documented selection process with conflict-of-interest controls.
Protect applicant records and restrict access to authorized personnel.

## Talent pathway
School education -> digital skills and projects -> higher education
or vocational certification -> internships -> employment,
research, or entrepreneurship.

Alternative pathways should remain available for different student needs.

## Key performance indicators
- Applications, awards, and student retention.
- Completion and certification rates.
- Internship placement and employment outcomes.
- Participation from underserved communities.
- Scholarship expenditure against approved budget.
- Graduate outcomes measured at defined intervals.

## Gate conditions
Confirm funding availability, selection governance, student support,
partner capacity, and multi-year financial sustainability before
making scholarship commitments.
