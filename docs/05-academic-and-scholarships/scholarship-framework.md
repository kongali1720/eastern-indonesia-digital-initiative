# Academic and Scholarship Framework

## Scholarship Objectives
Expand access while protecting the long-term financial sustainability
of the school.

## Proposed Controls
- Published eligibility and selection criteria.
- Documented application and review process.
- Conflict-of-interest declarations for selectors.
- Written award amount, duration, and renewal conditions.
- Separate records for restricted scholarship funds.
- Periodic reporting on awards, spending, retention, and outcomes.

## Academic Monitoring
Track enrollment, attendance, retention, learning outcomes, teacher
development, student wellbeing, and graduate pathways.

All criteria and targets require review before formal adoption.
