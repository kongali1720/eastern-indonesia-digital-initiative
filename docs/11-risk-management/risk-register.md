# Initial Risk Register

| Risk | Potential Impact | Initial Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Insufficient student demand | Revenue shortfall | Validate demand before capital commitment |
| Funding not secured | Delays or incomplete facilities | Use documented funding gates |
| Licensing delays | Opening postponed | Seek written authority guidance early |
| Construction cost escalation | Budget overrun | Independent estimates and contingency |
| Staff recruitment gaps | Quality and readiness issues | Develop staffing plan and hiring timeline |
| Scholarship funding shortfall | Student support interrupted | Track restricted funding and commitments |
| Data breach | Harm to students and legal exposure | Minimize data, control access, test security |
| Scope expansion | Financial and schedule pressure | Formal change control |
| Data center cross-subsidy | Unclear financial accountability | Separate budgets, contracts, and approvals |

Review likelihood, impact, owner, due date, and residual risk during each
formal project review.
