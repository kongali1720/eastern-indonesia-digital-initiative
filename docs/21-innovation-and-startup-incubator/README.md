# Innovation and Startup Incubator

## Purpose
Connect education and research with practical problem-solving,
entrepreneurship, and regional economic development.

## Proposed programs
- AI and data science laboratory.
- Robotics and Internet of Things projects.
- Software engineering and cybersecurity projects.
- Digital product prototyping and maker space.
- Startup mentoring and business-model development.
- Industry internships and applied research.
- Entrepreneurship workshops and demo days.

## Operating model
1. Identify regional or industry problems.
2. Select projects through transparent criteria.
3. Assign mentors and define measurable milestones.
4. Develop prototypes and test with authorized users.
5. Evaluate technical feasibility and market demand.
6. Decide whether to continue, revise, transfer, or close a project.

## Governance
Define intellectual-property ownership, student participation rules,
confidentiality, safety requirements, conflicts of interest, and
sponsor agreements before accepting external projects or investment.

## Key performance indicators
- Active projects and completion rate.
- Prototypes validated with intended users.
- Industry mentors and active partnerships.
- Internships and employment outcomes.
- Startups formed and sustained.
- External research or incubation funding secured.

## Gate conditions
Begin with a limited pilot program. Expand only when qualified mentors,
facilities, operating budgets, participant safeguards, and demand
have been validated.
