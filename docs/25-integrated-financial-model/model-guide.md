## How to use

1. Preserve the original assumptions and record source evidence for every change.
2. Have each workstream owner populate cost estimates and timing.
3. Reconcile each cost line against the master investment budget.
4. Identify unpriced items and unfunded commitments.
5. Separate proposed, committed, and received funding.
6. Populate cash-flow periods only when timing assumptions are defensible.
7. Run downside and upside scenarios after the base inputs are reviewed.
8. Record reviewer, date, version, and approval for each material change.

## Model discipline

Blank cells mean information is not yet available; they do not mean zero.
Do not present empty template values as forecasts.

The IDR 2.7 trillion total envelope is preliminary and unverified.
The IDR 1.1 trillion data-center allocation is included in that total.
Any revision must be reconciled and formally approved.
