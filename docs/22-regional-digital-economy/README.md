# Regional Digital Economy

## Purpose
Apply digital skills and infrastructure to real economic needs
across Eastern Indonesia.

## Candidate sectors
- Agriculture and food supply chains.
- Fisheries and marine-resource management.
- Micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs).
- Tourism and local creative industries.
- Logistics and regional commerce.
- Public-service and community information systems.

## Proposed interventions
- Digital bookkeeping and business-management training.
- E-commerce and digital marketing assistance.
- Inventory and supply-chain information tools.
- Data-driven agricultural and fisheries pilots.
- Local-language and accessible digital learning resources.
- Connectivity and digital-literacy partnerships.

## Selection criteria
Prioritize projects based on demonstrated community demand,
measurable benefits, affordability, data protection, local ownership,
operational sustainability, and potential for replication.

## Key performance indicators
- Participating organizations and active users.
- Training completion and demonstrated digital skills.
- Businesses adopting digital tools.
- User-reported improvements in productivity or access.
- Project continuation after initial support.
- Community satisfaction and accessibility.

## Safeguards
Obtain appropriate consent, minimize personal data collection,
avoid unsupported claims of economic impact, and evaluate projects
with participating communities.

## Gate conditions
Run small, measurable pilots before expanding to additional
districts or sectors. Do not assume projected economic benefits
will automatically materialize.
