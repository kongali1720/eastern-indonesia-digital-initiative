# Site and Facilities Planning

## Areas to Assess
- Classrooms and learning spaces.
- Computer laboratories and network infrastructure.
- Library and collaborative learning areas.
- Administration, teacher, and student-support facilities.
- Sanitation, accessibility, safety, and emergency access.
- Electricity, water, connectivity, backup power, and maintenance.
- Sports, recreation, and student wellbeing facilities.

## Required Evidence
Site survey, land due diligence, concept design, occupancy assumptions,
construction estimate, utility assessment, and maintenance plan.

No land purchase or construction commitment should rely only on this
preliminary folder or an unverified cost estimate.
